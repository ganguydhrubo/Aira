## What it is
X is a social platform, package `com.twitter.android`. Content is organised as posts; you can follow accounts, post, repost and send direct messages, and there is also Grok chat. The web version is x.com.

## Screens
There are five tabs at the bottom: Home, Explore, Grok, Notifications and Messages, with a floating post button at bottom right.

Home has the tabs "For you", "Following" and "+" (add). Under each post card are reply, repost, like, views, bookmark and share. Ads marked "Ad" are mixed into the feed. The "Show navigation drawer" button at the top opens the drawer menu, which has Profile, Premium, Communities, History, Lists, Spaces, Creator Studio, automation ("automated work"), Settings and privacy, Help Center and Display.

Explore has a search bar at the top, with tabs below: "For you", "Trending", "News", "Sports" and "Entertainment". Trends are grouped by topic or region (such as "Business & finance · Trending" or "Trending in Singapore"), and each item has a more options button on the right.

The profile page has the tabs "Posts", "Replies", "Reposts", "Videos" and "Articles"; at top right you can share and edit the profile.

## Features
- **Search**: open from the search bar on Explore; it shows recent searches with a clear option. The results page has "Top", "Latest", "People", "Media" and "Lists". Filters include From, date posted, Language, post engagement, exclude replies and Apply.
- **Read a post**: tapping a post opens its detail page, with a Follow button, the author and post options; next to the timestamp is the view count; below are reply, repost, like, bookmark and share. The reply box says "Post your reply" and lets you add photos or GIFs, or open the full editor. The page also has Grok's "Explain this post".
- **Create a post**: the floating button opens a full-screen editor. At the top you can switch account and set "Everyone can reply"; below are photos, camera, start a Space, GIF, poll, turn on location tag, content disclosure and add another post. The Post button cannot be tapped while the text is empty.
- **Notifications**: split into "All", "Mentions" and "Verified". There may be a banner saying notifications are turned off; notification settings are at top right.
- **Messages**: the chat list has an "All" drop-down and search at the top, and a new message button at bottom right. With no chats it says the inbox is empty, with suggested people and contact sync below.
- **Grok**: opened from the bottom tab. It is a chat screen where you can start a new chat and see chat history, with Voice mode and Auto mode.
- **Video**: tapping a video opens the full-screen player with pause, speed (1x), hide, floating (picture-in-picture) player and full screen, and a progress bar at the bottom.

## Known behaviour
Ads on Home carry a verified business badge and look just like normal posts. The post editor is a full-screen page; the system back key does not close it, so use the close button at top left.
