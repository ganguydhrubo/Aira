## What it is
LinkedIn is a professional networking app, package `com.linkedin.android`.

## Known behaviour
- When not signed in, it opens on a welcome page: rotating intro cards, "Sign in with email", and "New to LinkedIn? Join now".
- A Google "Sign in to LinkedIn" bottom panel ("Continue as ...") may pop up over the welcome page first. This panel belongs to Google services and has no readable elements; close it with the "×" at top right.
