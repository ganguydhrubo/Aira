## What it is
Uber Eats is a food delivery app, package `com.ubercab.eats`.

## Screens
It has Home/Search, restaurant menus, Cart and Orders. Restaurant food is a separate section from Grocery, Convenience, Retail and so on.

## Features
- **Restaurants**: shown for the delivery address, with name, rating, reviews, estimated delivery time, delivery fee, offers, open/closed status and link. There are two modes, Delivery and Pickup. Closed restaurants have no estimated delivery time.
- **Menu**: dish names, categories, prices in local currency, options and whether sold out. Usually visible without signing in.
- **Cart**: shows options chosen, quantity, minimum order, each fee, the address and door/flat details, and the total. Each tap on Add adds one item.
- **Orders**: Orders has past orders and the delivery progress of current orders.

## Known behaviour
- Restaurants and supermarket/retail items are in different sections.
- Sign-in or verification screens may appear.
