## What it is
WhatsApp is a messaging app, package `com.whatsapp`. For the website, see `whatsapp.com`.

## Screens
It has Chats, Contacts, search and a new chat entry. Search results are split into Chats, Contacts and Messages. Contact details can be opened by category, or found in the contact list under New Chat.

## Features
- **Read messages**: open the chat to read the content and times. In a group, if you are not sure who a member is, treat them as unknown.
- **Send messages**: after choosing the contact, type the text, send it, and check the sending status in the chat.

## Known behaviour
- Phone number verification and linking by scanning a QR code in a browser are two different things.
- The app language follows the system language and may not be English.
