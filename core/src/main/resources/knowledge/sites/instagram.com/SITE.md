## What it is
A photo and short-video social platform with profiles, the feed, search and explore, posts, Reels and direct messages (Messages).

## Pages and URLs
Home `https://www.instagram.com/`. A profile is at `/<username>/`.

## Features
- **Profile**: username, bio, post count, follower count, following count, verified badge and private status. Display names can repeat; only the username is unique.
- **Posts and Reels**: author, caption, time posted, like and comment counts. Reach them from an account search, a hashtag or a post link.

## Known behaviour
Private account pages and rate-limited pages both look as if they have no content.
